What to check before shipping

Save the original seller URL, selected color and size, seller measurements, estimated item price and quote date for each product you shortlist.

From listing to warehouse

  1. Open the CAFANS product page and follow its original seller link. Confirm that the variant you want can still be selected.
  2. If you plan to use OrientDig, check whether it accepts the seller URL and ships to your destination. Confirm any restrictions and fees before paying.
  3. When warehouse photos are available, check that they show your exact variant. Ask for a close-up or ruler photo if a detail affects your decision.
  4. Check the return deadline before dispatch. If an item is wrong, ask the agent about the seller’s return or exchange options while it is still at the warehouse.

QC checks by category

  • Shoes: Compare both size labels, outsole wear or defects, and insole length against a pair you already own.
  • Hoodies and tees: Compare flat chest width and body length. Ask for close-ups of embroidery or prints that look misaligned.
  • Jackets: Verify sleeve and shoulder measurements and ask whether the zipper closes cleanly and the lining has visible tears.

OrientDig spreadsheet FAQ

What is an OrientDig spreadsheet?

It is a shopping shortlist that organizes seller links, variants, prices and inspection notes for people considering an order through OrientDig. This independent guide uses CAFANS product listings for the catalog.

Where can I see QC photos?

Check whether your shopping agent provides warehouse photos for your order. Seller images in the catalog show the listing. Use warehouse photos to review the specific item received and request additional angles if available.

Why might the final price differ from the catalog price?

The seller’s current price, selected variant, exchange rate and agent charges can affect the amount due. International shipping and any taxes or duties can increase the full cost of delivery.

How do I compare shipping quotes for bulky items?

Ask each shipping line for its billable weight and whether it charges by actual weight, dimensional weight or another rule. Use the same packed dimensions for each quote and check what insurance and delivery services are included.
